About Us · Since 2016

Made for the whole table

Jollof House began with one family recipe and a deep love for the generous, joyful food we grew up sharing. Our kitchen layers smoky firewood flavour, bright peppers, and patient cooking into every plate.

Come for a quick bowl of pepper soup or settle in with friends. There is always room for one more at our table.
